# PulseIQ Attendance & Leave — Insights Report

## Overview

This report summarises three unusual months identified by comparing monthly attendance rates against the median attendance rate across the analysed period.

**Median attendance rate:** 94.11%

## Insight 1: July 2025 — Above-median attendance

* Attendance rate: 95.51%
* Difference from median: 1.41 percentage points above
* Late arrivals: 556
* Overtime hours: 589.0

**Finding:** Attendance was comparatively strong, but late arrivals and overtime remained notable. Reviewing punctuality and overtime alongside attendance may help identify opportunities to improve workforce scheduling.

## Insight 2: September 2025 — Above-median attendance

* Attendance rate: 95.48%
* Difference from median: 1.37 percentage points above
* Late arrivals: 568
* Overtime hours: 554.0

**Finding:** Attendance remained high, while late arrivals increased slightly compared with July. Monitoring punctuality and overtime can help assess whether strong attendance is being maintained sustainably.

## Insight 3: May 2026 — Below-median attendance

* Attendance rate: 92.89%
* Difference from median: 1.22 percentage points below
* Late arrivals: 476
* Overtime hours: 492.5

**Finding:** May showed a comparatively low attendance rate. The lower attendance coincided with fewer recorded late arrivals and less overtime than in July and September 2025. Further investigation of absence and leave patterns would help explain the decline.

## Methodology and limitations

* Attendance rate was calculated as (present days + 0.5 × unpaid half-days) divided by scheduled days.
* Unusual months were selected based on the absolute difference between monthly attendance and the median attendance rate.
* The figures shown are aggregated across departments.
* Late arrivals and overtime provide additional context but do not, by themselves, explain changes in attendance.
* These results use the full attendance dataset and may differ from the dashboard when department or month filters are changed.
